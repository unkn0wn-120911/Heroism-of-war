# Heroism of War - Game Design Outline

## Genre

3D action-adventure shooter with battle royale arena structure, vehicle segments, and cooperative progression.

## Pillars

1. Fast combat loop
2. Tactical movement
3. Vehicle and racing segments
4. Loot and progression
5. Multiplayer session structure

## Player systems

- Health, armor, stamina, and ammo management
- Weapon categories: rifle, SMG, shotgun, sniper
- Ability cards or perks per match
- Local and online squad system

## Enemy systems

- Patrol AI for ground enemies
- Aggro detection and chase behavior
- Wave-based spawn pressure
- Boss encounter for end-of-match threat

## World design

- Desert-style battleground with cover and vertical sightlines
- City block map with alleyways and rooftops
- Crash zone for vehicle pickup
- Dynamic shrinking safe ring similar to battle royale flow

## Database layer

- Account profile: username, rank, wins, losses
- Match history: score, survival time, kills
- Inventory and weapon skin progression
- Save sessions for multiplayer lobbies

## Roadmap

- Phase 1: core combat prototype
- Phase 2: map and enemy AI
- Phase 3: vehicle driving and racing
- Phase 4: multiplayer lobby and MongoDB sync
- Phase 5: feature polish, optimization, and production packaging
